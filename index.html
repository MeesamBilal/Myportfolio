Final Year Project

AI Smart Glasses for the visually impaired.

A wearable I designed and built with real hardware: a camera on the frame sees the scene, AI describes it, and a speaker reads it aloud, with visual-to-audio latency under 2 seconds.

Introduction

Over 285 million people worldwide, including about 2.6 million in Pakistan, live with visual impairment (WHO, 2023). Existing devices like Envision and OrCam are expensive and lack Urdu support, and local versions are bulky and limited. AI Smart Glasses offer an affordable, lightweight, bilingual alternative for object detection, text reading and voice guidance.

Problem statement

Most visually impaired people in Pakistan cannot afford existing assistive devices. Local solutions are bulky, single-purpose and not user-friendly. The need: an affordable, lightweight, bilingual AI wearable that detects objects, reads text and gives real-time voice guidance.
